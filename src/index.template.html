Made by hand

Every piece starts on a small workbench: beads sorted by colour, wire cut to length, clay rolled and cut by hand. Small runs mean slight differences between pieces — a bead sitting a fraction differently, a wrap turned a little tighter. That is the point of it.

Orders are packed and posted within three working days.

Looking after it

Handmade pieces last a long time with very little effort.

Keep pieces away from water, perfume and hand cream — put jewellery on last, take it off first. Store earrings in their own pouch so posts don't bend and stones don't scratch.

Wipe silver with a soft dry cloth if it dulls. Don't use liquid polish on plated or beaded work; it strips the finish. Keep magnets away from cards, watches and laptops.

Anything that arrives damaged gets replaced or refunded — message within seven days of delivery with a photo.

Made-to-order and personalised pieces can't be returned, so check sizing before ordering.
